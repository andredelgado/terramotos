---
title: Faial, 1998
summary: Off the north-east coast of Faial, 9 July 1998
---

Before dawn on 9 July 1998 an earthquake struck a few kilometres off the north-east coast of Faial.

Eight people died, more than a hundred were injured and well over a thousand lost their homes. The parishes of north-eastern Faial, among them Ribeirinha, Pedro Miguel and Salão, were hit hardest, with damage on Pico and São Jorge as well.

Many of the traditional stone houses did not survive, and rebuilding took years. The aftershocks went on for weeks: open the map to see them gather off the island’s coast.
